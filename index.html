Plain English guide
Momentum (RSI 0–100): Like a speedometer. Below 30 = too slow, stock may bounce up. Above 70 = too fast, may slow down/drop. 30–70 = healthy normal speed.

Price vs 20-day average: The average price of the last 20 days. If today is above that average = stock is strong. Below = weak.

20-day forecast: If the current trend continues at the same speed, this is where price may go. Not guaranteed.

Risk score: 1–3 = low risk, good to invest. 4–6 = medium, invest carefully. 7–10 = high risk, avoid buying now.
